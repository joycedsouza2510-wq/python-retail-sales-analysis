# Retail Sales Analysis with Python

## Project Overview

This project analyses retail sales data using Python, Pandas and Matplotlib. It explores sales revenue, profit, units sold, product performance and regional performance, and uses the results to develop business insights and recommendations.

The project demonstrates how Python can be used to analyse retail data, calculate key performance indicators (KPIs), identify sales trends and communicate findings to support business decisions.

## Business Questions

- What are the overall sales and profit KPIs?
- Which products generate the most revenue?
- Which regions contribute the most revenue?
- What business opportunities can be identified from the results?

## Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

- **Total Revenue:** €8,625.00
- **Total Profit:** €4,183.00
- **Total Units Sold:** 37
- **Profit Margin:** 48.50%

### KPI Definitions

- **Total Revenue:** The total sales value generated by all orders.
- **Total Profit:** Revenue minus the product costs included in the analysis.
- **Total Units Sold:** The total quantity of products sold.
- **Profit Margin:** Total profit divided by total revenue, multiplied by 100.

Based on the supplied figures, the business generated approximately €48.50 in profit for every €100 in revenue. If operating expenses and overheads are excluded, this should be described as gross profit margin rather than net profit margin.

### Product Findings

1. Televisions generated the highest revenue at €4,920.00, representing 57.04% of total revenue.
2. Soundbars ranked second, generating €2,520.00, or 29.22% of total revenue.
3. Signal boosters generated €660.00 in revenue.
4. HDMI cables generated the lowest revenue at €525.00.
5. Televisions and Soundbars together generated €7,440.00, accounting for approximately 86.26% of total revenue.

## Business Recommendations

- Monitor Television stock and customer demand because Televisions contribute the largest share of revenue.
- Explore opportunities to cross-sell Soundbars with Televisions, as these two products account for approximately 86.26% of revenue.
- Investigate product demand and sales opportunities in Cork and Galway.
- Compare revenue with product-level costs and profit before making decisions about pricing, promotions or inventory.
- Continue tracking revenue, profit, units sold and profit margin to monitor business performance over time.

## Limitations

The KPIs and revenue breakdowns in this README are based on the figures supplied for this project. Product-level profit and regional profit breakdowns have not been included here, so no conclusions are made about which individual product or region is most profitable.

The analysis would benefit from a larger dataset covering a longer period. Results should be checked against the underlying dataset to ensure that all notebook outputs reconcile with the reported figures.
